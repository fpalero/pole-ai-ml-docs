# Magic Links & Temp Access: Consumer Identity

**Section:** AI Applications

## What it delivers
Consumer-grade authentication for end users — not just employees — that scales
down to a single developer.

## How it's built
Keycloak SMTP verify-email magic links with a dev Mailpit sandbox, short-lived
temp-access sessions, a GDPR data purge, and credential separation via Helm
secrets.

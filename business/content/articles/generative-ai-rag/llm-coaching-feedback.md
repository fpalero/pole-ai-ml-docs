# LLM Coaching Feedback from Metrics

**Section:** Generative AI & RAG · **Featured:** ✅

## What it delivers
AI coaching that turns raw metrics into human-readable insight — contextual
tips and improvement plans a user can actually act on.

## How it's built
Z-score outliers against a cohort flag flaws, critical-frame images and
deviation plots attach, and an LLM writes contextual tips plus 4-week
improvement plans.

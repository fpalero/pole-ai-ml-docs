# Why Your Project Needs ADRs

**Section:** AI Applications

## What it delivers
Institutional memory — six months later, you still know *why* a decision was
made, and new engineers (or agents) onboard from the record.

## How it's built
Decision-record discipline via real examples: ADR templates, a decisions tree,
and records that double as onboarding material and agent fodder.

# Model Persistence, Versioning & the Retraining Toolkit

**Section:** Machine Learning

## What it delivers
MLOps for a lean team — version your model like a data artifact, with a clear
path to retrain when the world changes.

## How it's built
`.keras`/SavedModel persistence with metadata, and the retraining routes
compared: fine-tune existing, few-shot assist, and threshold steering.

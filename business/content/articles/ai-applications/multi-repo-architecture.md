# Multi-Repo Architecture Without Monorepo Pain

**Section:** AI Applications

## What it delivers
Code, docs, and infrastructure split into separate repos without the chaos —
clear rules so a growing team never cross-pushes.

## How it's built
An absolute path-to-repo routing table, worktree feature branches, and
develop/main policies that keep each repo's ownership unambiguous.

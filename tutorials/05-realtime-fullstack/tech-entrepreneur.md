## Catalog

### E3 (product lens) — "Your Coach, Always On": The Realtime Coaching Product
- **Difficulty:** Any
- **Type:** Product narrative
- **Hook:** "Dartfish and Hudl charge studios; here's the open-source path to the same product."
- **Description:** The coaching-product story: quantitative flaw detection,
  critical-frame images, improvement plans — positioned against commercial
  biomechanics software. Great for founders in sports/technique-training tech.
- **Grounding:** `docs/app/pola_agent/pose_correction.md`, `docs/app/pole_api/phase-26-analyst-coach-tools/PAIML-POLE-API-076.md`.

### E1 (product lens) — Latency Is a Feature: Real-Time as an Upsell
- **Difficulty:** Any
- **Type:** Product/technical explainer
- **Hook:** "Less than 100 ms isn't an engineering nicety — it's the difference between feedback and interruption."
- **Description:** The business meaning of the sub-100 ms budget and live
  consensus: session feel, coaching-in-the-moment value, and where real-time
  earns users.
- **Grounding:** `docs/diagrams/pola_agent/FLOW.md`.
- **Sellable angle:** Connects performance budgets to pricing value.

### E6 — Design Tokens → Real UI: An AI-Generated Angular Frontend
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Type:** Product/technical guide
- **Hook:** "Let AI generate the screens — keep it consistent with design tokens."
- **Description:** From a token-driven design system (Stitch) to a working
  Angular app: lazy-loaded feature modules, signal-based state, chart
  components for biomech analysis, and a resilient WebSocket chat UI. Why
  token discipline makes AI-generated UI look designed, not generated.
- **Grounding:** `docs/app/pole_analyst/fe_design.md`, `docs/app/pole_fe/c_UI_design_common.md`, `docs/app/pole_fe/phase-9-biomech-analysis/PAIML-POLE-FE-007.md`.
- **Sellable angle:** Hot intersection (AI UI generation + real product) with a
  visual payoff that screenshots well.

# Keycloak as the Identity Layer

**Section:** AI Applications · **Featured:** ✅

## What it delivers
Authentication done right — a reproducible, source-controlled identity layer
instead of hand-rolled auth you'll have to fix later.

## How it's built
Keycloak realm JSON and themes kept in the repo as the source of truth,
magic-link auth, temp-access sessions, and the ConfigMap trap fixed with a
checksum annotation.

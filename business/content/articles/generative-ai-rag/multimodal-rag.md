# Multimodal RAG with Image Descriptions

**Section:** Generative AI & RAG · **Featured:** ✅

## What it delivers
Retrieval that understands pictures, not just prose — so users can query by
meaning across documents *and* their figures.

## How it's built
Two collections, one store: text chunks and image descriptions embedded side
by side in ChromaDB, with full-rebuild semantics for reproducible indexing.

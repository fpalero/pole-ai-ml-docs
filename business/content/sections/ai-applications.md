# Section 1 — AI Applications

## Service description (outcome-first)

Ship a full-stack AI product — backend, frontend, and the infrastructure to run
it — without the "works on my laptop" gap. I take an AI product from a working
prototype to a deployed, monitored, production system: FastAPI backends,
Angular frontends, job queues, identity, and Kubernetes deployment. Real
products, not demos.

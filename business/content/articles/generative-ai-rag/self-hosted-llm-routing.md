# Self-Hosted OpenAI-Compatible LLM Routing

**Section:** Generative AI & RAG · **Featured:** ✅

## What it delivers
Your own model router for CI and internal tools — keep prompt traffic in-house
and cut per-call cost with an OpenAI-compatible `/v1` surface.

## How it's built
Two auth tiers, runtime API-key provisioning, and a keyless mode for ephemeral
jobs, all behind an OpenAI-compatible interface.

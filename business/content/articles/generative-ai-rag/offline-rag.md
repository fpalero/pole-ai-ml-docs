# Zero-Cost Offline Documentation RAG

**Section:** Generative AI & RAG · **Featured:** ✅

## What it delivers
A retrieval system that answers from your documents with **zero per-call API
cost** — the economic alternative every budget-conscious team asks for.

## How it's built
Local all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings, ChromaDB persistence, incremental
hash-manifest writes versus full rebuilds, and per-chunk metadata for
reproducible retrieval.

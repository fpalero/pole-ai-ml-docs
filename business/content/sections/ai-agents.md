# Section 2 — AI Agents

## Service description (outcome-first)

Specialized AI agents that answer from your data, call your tools, and never go
off-script — built with RAG, tool-calling, and deterministic guardrails. I
design agents that do real work safely: bounded ReAct loops, multi-agent
graphs, and guardrails that survive prompt drift.

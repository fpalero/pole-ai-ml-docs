# Training Studio: Launching Long ML Jobs from a Browser

**Section:** AI Applications

## What it delivers
A job launcher with real UX — so a non-ML team member can kick off training
with clear expectations, instead of a developer running a script.

## How it's built
Radio mode cards, multi-class checkboxes with per-class video counts,
data-balance warnings, and confirm dialogs that set expectations before epoch
progress streams live.
